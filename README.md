## How to play

**New falling-sky restoration visits:** Swipe neighboring stars to arrange a matching 2×2 square of four, a cross of five, or a straight horizontal/vertical run of four or more. Retained revision-3 rounds require seven for a straight line. Patterns clear automatically after a swap or shower; falling survivors can form another pattern and build a chain. Count actual stars, including each grown star once. Overlapping patterns clear each shared star once. Taps inspect without clearing, and traces do not activate clears. Reach the location’s announced point objective before an arriving star has nowhere to land.

**Open Sky and retained manual rounds:** Use the tap, trace and setup controls below. Tutorial starts with a current Story setup; its other chapters cover Open Sky and historical restoration controls. Continue and same-round Replay keep each saved definition; an explicit new visit selects the current rules.

- **Clear a match:** Horizontally or vertically connected matching jewels must total at least 3 light units. Normal jewels count as 1; larger jewels with a soft aura count as 2. Their soft aura gently pulses in normal motion. In retained Dusk rounds, clear beside Dusk to dissolve it early; gray neighbors still match and grow, but score zero until released.
- **Keep playing:** Ready groups stay interactive while other columns settle. One recent tap or swap can wait briefly for its stars, with a visible outline/arrow acknowledgment. A new touch replaces that intention; changed boards and interruptions cancel it. Short ordinary finger drift stays a tap.
- **Draw a constellation:** Hold a star until it glows, then draw through five or more matching stars, beside one another or across one empty space in a straight line, including diagonals, then lift. Only the stars you traced clear; retrace to branch. Drawing holds the next shower until release or cancellation.
- **Arrange a setup:** Swipe left, right, up or down to exchange neighbors immediately in Free Play and restoration challenges, including automatic-pattern rounds. Each touch moves one space regardless of color. Stars exchanged into space fall normally. Hold then trace exact four-star squares or five-star crosses; surrounding stars stay. Build the next recipe, then clear again for a deliberate relay. [Techniques](docs/design/STAR_TECHNIQUES.md).
- **Understand the sky:** Characters explain play, storm counts and timing warnings in one bottom chat. Tap the retained portrait to reread a message. Six conversation portraits and bundled scripts share one reusable presentation; [edit or preview dialogue](App/DialogueScripts/README.md).
- **Control the showers:** Call the next shower yourself or clear while paused. Use the sky menu to pause or tune shower pace (retained Dusk rounds also keep dark-star frequency); Start/Resume and manual advance appear while paused.
- **Restore a place:** Choose **Story Mode**, then select a ready destination on the world map. New Lantern visits have a 150,000-point target and gentle showers; new Ribbon visits have a 220,000-point target and steady showers. Each has its own authored opening and supply. New Kite visits have a compact 17,000-point headroom-versus-points opening and gentle showers. New Seed visits use an 11,000-point grown-ingredient encounter and gentle showers. New Silver visits use a 12,000-point causal-sequence encounter and gentle showers. New Frostfruit visits use a 15,000-point top-growth versus immediate-reward encounter and gentle showers. New Sunwheel visits combine those skills through a 24,000-point objective and gentle showers. Historical revision-0 saves and same-round Replay retain 18-light targets and steady showers. All seven omit dark stars. Previewing a choice preserves the current round until Begin; replay keeps that round's rules, while New sky returns to survival. When an arrival column has no room, replay or start a new sky.

## Open and run

Requires Xcode 16+ with an iOS 17+ simulator runtime. Open `Starfall.xcodeproj`, select shared scheme **Starfall** and an installed iPhone simulator, then Run. Simulator builds do not require paid Apple enrollment or a signing team. Local development signing has been exercised on a connected iPhone; choose your own Personal Team in Xcode. Distribution signing is not configured. This remains a prototype; App Store distribution and submission metadata require their own release work.

## Scope

[The adoption register](docs/EXPERIMENTS.md) distinguishes shipping gameplay from isolated trials. Falling sky and its physical reactions are adopted in both Debug and Release; supernova recoloring and alternate shower candidates remain outside the game.

- **Rules and saves:** Pure deterministic core, Codable state and on-device saving after committed events. Replay or New sky resets the round.
- **Native presentation:** SwiftUI screens/settings and a SpriteKit board with seven faceless piece families; original expressive characters serve the story. The original Debug puzzle keeps its crystal artwork and procedural fallback.
- **Bounded motion:** Clear fragments, downward settling, diagonal refill, angled arrivals, weighted rebounds and survivor ripples (stronger at 5+ starlight). These present committed outcomes. [Connected contact](docs/evidence/star-contact-2026-10-17/README.md) adds local weight transfer, finite neighbor reactions, surface light and a quiet resting sky.
- **Feedback and accessibility:** A luminous drawn thread, brief star sparks and score popup; local sound/haptics on for new preferences (saved off is preserved; sound respects Silent Mode); Calm effects honors Reduce Motion. The VoiceOver alternative grid exposes row/column, jewel name and connected-group size.

The shared [gameplay material factory](docs/design/star-cast/README.md) supplies the board and flight with shell materials and bounded inner-light responses; the HUD uses cached resting composites. Seven shared family shaders keep each piece's response independent through per-node attributes. A separate bounded character cache retains the original expressive cast for story and ceremony. Neither needs an external runtime dependency.
