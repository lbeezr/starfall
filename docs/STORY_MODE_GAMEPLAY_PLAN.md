# Give Story Mode distinct decisions

**Status:** Lantern/Ribbon revision-4 four-line trial development-verified and installed. Later encounters and ratings remain proposed · **Owner:** Current implementer · **Updated:** October 22, 2026

Make Story Mode a progression from a welcoming introduction to expressive arcade mastery, guided by [Pokémon Puzzle League](design/POKEMON_PUZZLE_LEAGUE_REFERENCE.md). Give each location distinct decisions, theatrical scores and celebrations, with optional mastery proposed for replay. Current completed repairs open the next ready location; ratings are not implemented. Keep automatic showers, pause and manual advance; design substantial rounds without a clock that prevents an earned finish.

**Next action:** Follow the [current roadmap](ROADMAP.md): continue the existing experience queue, observe the [four-star-line trial](evidence/four-star-lines-2026-10-22/README.md), and prepare the [Kite/curriculum](https://github.com/lbeezr/starfall/issues/252), [mastery contract](design/STORY_MASTERY_CONTRACT.md) and [Bell Choir/content budget](design/BELL_CHOIR_AND_CAMPAIGN_BUDGET.md). The later five ready places retain 18-light rules. Target/time estimates and manual-rule mastery examples below remain historical proposals, not ready current-pattern acceptance.

## Longer rounds need more decisions

The original [progression research](design/MAHJONG_PROGRESSION.md#current-tranche-and-later-extension) calls 18 provisional, intended to demonstrate a completion/retry flow before a campaign existed. It has not been established as the right campaign length.

The user has clarified that large numbers, dramatic celebrations and Pokémon Puzzle League's arcade energy are central to the intended experience. The earlier 60/90/120/150/180 proposal and its short duration budgets are superseded.

Use **1,000 points per ordinary light unit and 2,000 for a grown star** as the base scoring scale. Before bonuses, three light units are worth **3,000**, five small stars **5,000**, and twelve light units **12,000**. Revised Lantern/Ribbon clears also add 1,000 for each actual star beyond three, so five small stars award **7,000** before any causal multiplier. Revision 2 multiplies that phase award by its depth, capped at ×5; a new action resets to ×1. Growth still stores two incoming units in one occupied cell. Do not multiply stored legacy scores or silently change their completed outcomes.

Changing the score scale creates numerical spectacle. Increasing the underlying content and goal separately creates a longer round. The following table does both:

| Location | Proposed displayed target | Base light equivalent before bonuses | First-clear active-play budget to test |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Lantern Landing | 150,000 | 150 | Roughly 4–5 minutes |
| Ribbon Crossing | 220,000 | 220 | Roughly 5–6 minutes |
| Kite Harbor | 300,000 | 300 | Roughly 6–7 minutes |
| Seed Moon | 360,000 | 360 | Roughly 6–8 minutes |
| Silver Pools | 450,000 | 450 | Roughly 7–9 minutes |

These point/time pairs have not been validated against new supply or human play. They are first-clear pacing hypotheses, not minimum times or predictions from simulation. The implemented Lantern six-second and Ribbon four-second cadence is the current baseline; user feedback reports too little activity. Neither those intervals nor the subsequent roughly two-second suggestion is a research-backed recommendation for the revision. [Competitive research](design/PUZZLE_PACING_RESEARCH.md) now sequences opening workload, supply composition and measured speed separately. Fast recognition and manual showers may produce shorter rounds. Let skill shorten a clear.

Author enough meaningful play to fill each budget: a readable opening, changed situations, opportunities to recover and a final combination of skills. Avoid making duration mostly a wait for enough incoming light. Measure useful decisions, empty stretches and recognition as well as elapsed time. The existing five paintings continue to follow committed progress; at 150,000 the visual thresholds are 0/37,500/75,000/112,500/150,000. A large clear can skip a painting without delaying the win.

If a target produces repetitive play or a long uneventful middle, improve the encounters, split the location into distinct rounds or shorten it. Never hold a completed board open, cap an earned award, slow supply after good play or insert mandatory waiting merely to hit a duration.

## Give skill and spectacle room to grow

Use the existing Crystal Comet lettering, cast, light collection and audio owners. Aggregate each clear into one exact large award in the protected award region. Let ordinary clears feel crisp, major clears have a stronger impact and collection sweep, causal relays build a musical/visual phrase, and restoration resolve into a larger finale. Keep the board readable and the next move available throughout decorative tails. Quiet modes retain exact scores and milestones.

Classify celebrations by the underlying action and award tier, not by the new raw numeric magnitude; otherwise multiplying all scores would make every ordinary clear trigger an exceptional celebration. Maintain contrast between a routine +3,000 and a genuinely unusual play. Group exact totals such as 150,000, and verify six-figure goals, overflow awards and largest-text layouts.

The earlier blanket preference for flat per-star rewards is reopened. Puzzle League's reference makes it worth testing whether a strong selected clear or a causal relay should earn an additional, bounded reward. Compare the scaled base score against one small earned-bonus candidate using existing actions. Only actual new clears/consumed ingredients can qualify; repeated swaps, selections and unrelated quick taps cannot farm a bonus. A causal relay can remain valid through a pause.

The first slice selected the extra-star bonus described above; revision 2 also implements causal phase multipliers from ×1 through ×5. Recalibrate targets and durations with those rules. Conserved star light and earned bonuses remain separately validatable inside Core while presenting one score. An alternative pressure-relief reward remains deferred; the six-/four-second shower cadence is unchanged in this revision. Implementation does not establish balanced rewards or satisfying duration.

**October 20 follow-up:** The user confirmed automatic patterns only for new Lantern/Ribbon visits. Revision 3 retires tap and selective trace clears in those rounds and adds full seven-star lines. The earlier activation, banking and mastery proposals below are historical design exploration, not requirements to retain manual clears. Pattern construction and causal falling determine rewards; unrelated moves never accumulate a timed combo.

The same committed clear receives the same score through a tap, trace or accessible action. A bonus for drawing an already tappable set would reward extra input rather than a better result. Check duplicate awards and splitting exploits before adopting any size/relay formula.

## Twelve places need an explicit content budget

The first-five pacing targets total **28–35 minutes** of successful active play. For a one-round-per-place campaign, a provisional remaining-seven budget of 7–9, 8–10, 8–10, 8–10, 9–11, 9–12 and 10–14 minutes totals another 59–76. All twelve would therefore provide **87–111 minutes** before retries, reading, story or optional replay. This arithmetic is a scope estimate, not measured playtime. Frostfruit Orchard and Sunwheel Mill are built with their historical rules; five locations remain unbuilt, and all seven places beyond the first-five briefs still need distinct gameplay proposals.

That is a short campaign. Long late rounds also risk tiring repetition. For a several-hour journey, the recommended expansion is **three or four genuinely different repair rounds per location**, rather than twelve increasingly long score bars:

| Content model | Successful active-play estimate |
| --- | --- |
| 12 single rounds, using the budgets above | 87–111 minutes |
| 3 rounds per location: 36 rounds averaging 4–6 minutes | 144–216 minutes |
| 4 rounds per location: 48 rounds averaging 5–7½ minutes | 240–360 minutes |

The natural per-location arc is introduction, variation, combination and capstone. Each round needs different authored decisions and its own clear finish. A practiced player can complete it faster; optional mastery, difficulty variants and multiplayer are additional possibilities, not guaranteed hours used to justify the story estimate.

This expanded content model is a recommendation for the next scope decision, not authorization to build 48 boards now. Validate the first location pair before multiplying production. If adopted, define round-level completion, shared location restoration and replay persistence explicitly before implementation; do not silently turn today's one-round repair into a multi-round gate.

## Future local versus and cooperative restoration

The user wants the feeling of nearby Game Boys or couch play: two iPhones/iPads on the same local network, joining a local session without accounts or online matchmaking.

- **Local versus:** Separate boards, clear shared stakes and opportunities for combos/relays to affect the opponent. Study Puzzle League's attack and recovery relationship before choosing Starfall's interaction.
- **Local cooperation:** Work together to restore one location. First compare linked personal boards contributing to a shared objective with a truly shared board; establish how one player's useful action helps the other. A shared score alone is not sufficient evidence of cooperation.

Both are intended future directions, not current implementation work. Keep the present app offline. Its deterministic Core and committed actions are useful foundations; add no speculative transport framework, lobby, network permission or SDK in this batch. A later feature plan must settle session authority, interruptions/rejoin, fairness and explicit sharing of repair credit while preserving each device's existing progress. No remote service, account or paid infrastructure is proposed.

## The five location briefs

**Historical manual-rule examples.** Their decision themes remain useful, but taps, selective traces and banking completed patterns cannot be copied into current automatic-pattern encounters. [#252](https://github.com/lbeezr/starfall/issues/252) develops a version-aware curriculum and witnessed Kite brief; the roadmap owns current sequencing.

All encounters use current matching, growth, cardinal exchanges, selected traces, exact recipes, settling and deliberate follow-ups. They use ordinary colored stars and grown stars. The first pass adds no new piece, power, obstacle, aging, Dusk, currency or move limit. Opal and storm rules remain available in their existing contexts; they are unnecessary to establish this five-location curriculum.

Author openings and a versioned deterministic sequence of legal arrivals. Reuse useful scenario ideas from the tutorial, but let the player choose different valid solutions. Never reset the board between beats, teleport pieces, alter an already announced shower or require the tutorial's exact action sequence.

| Location | Central decision | Opening → development → finish | What makes the choice useful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lantern Landing: recognize** | Clear a ready group now, or keep a useful matching top star while clearing elsewhere. | An obvious ordinary match lights the wick. Two available families let the player choose their next clear. Matching arrivals demonstrate compact two-light growth. A larger clear can finish the lantern, with small clears remaining a safe route. | The player sees that their choices change both light and room. Basic taps must suffice for an approachable completion; mastery is optional. |
| **Ribbon Crossing: connect** | Join separated stars with a swap or trace, or take a nearby simple match and preserve those ingredients. | An almost-connected family becomes useful after one exchange. Later, a diagonal or single-empty-space connection offers a five-star trace across separate ordinary groups. Near the finish, taking that selected connection leaves a different board from spending a whole group. | Connecting can harvest otherwise unavailable light or release useful supports. The alternative remains recoverable through later supply. |
| **Kite Harbor: prepare** | Take the largest immediate award, or open space in a crowded part of the board. | Uneven arrivals create two busy columns and a quieter region. A small clear in a tall column competes with a larger low-column clear. Later, one well-chosen clear can open room in two columns. The final sequence reuses that choice with a different arrangement. | Headroom and immediate light pull in different directions. Showers give the cleared space a purpose without demanding a last-second rescue. |
| **Seed Moon: preserve** | Spend a whole family now, or leave useful grown ingredients for a better follow-up. | Repeated colors make compression visible. A ready group can be kept while another part of the board is cleared. Later, overlapping shapes let an exact selection leave a grown star and matching survivors. The final choice combines banking with a useful release. | What remains after a clear matters. Growth's benefit is storage in fewer cells; no passive interest or score multiplier is implied. |
| **Silver Pools: sequence** | Choose the order of clears so that falling survivors create the next opportunity. | A support clear forms a square. A fork offers more light now or a useful follow-up. The final problem allows a first clear to create two subsequent matches, with a simpler recovery path still available. | The player plans consequences across two or three committed clears. The water-garden setting reinforces connected steps without adding water mechanics. |

Kite must communicate its choices from visible column heights. The current compact forecast shows incoming star materials but is not a reliable visual map of receiving columns. Do not grade knowledge of hidden column assignments. If exact forecast placement becomes essential to an encounter, first add and verify a legible native and accessible placement cue.

Each signature situation must support at least two materially different responses. Record a before/after example showing different survivors, available matches or column room; a named recipe with an identical consequence is insufficient evidence of a meaningful choice.

Supply must offer another route to a missed accomplishment before the target. Do not make an optional star silently impossible because the player took an ordinary opening clear. Do not insert an unannounced rescue or change an existing preview to manufacture another chance.

## Three stars reward accomplishments

Borrow the separation between finishing a course and pursuing extra accomplishments. Nintendo's [Captain Toad manual](https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/games_8/emanuals/wii_u_6/captain_toad__treasure_tracker/ElectronicManual_WiiU_CaptainToadTreasureTracker_EN.pdf), “Selecting Courses,” distinguishes clear status, collected gems and a bonus objective. That is a useful reference for optional mastery; Starfall's precise rating below is our proposal.

| Result | Requirement in one successful run |
| --- | --- |
| ★ | Restore the place. |
| ★★ | Restore it and complete either local accomplishment. |
| ★★★ | Restore it and complete both. |

One star is a complete success: full restoration, normal ending and access to the next ready place. Additional stars are replay goals. Avoid grading a first clear “poor” or “only okay.” There is no total-star unlock requirement.

Show both goals before Begin and in the pause menu. Keep the main HUD focused on restoration; acknowledge an accomplishment briefly without covering the board. After Lantern's first completion, explain the optional replay challenge gently. Goals may be earned incidentally on the first attempt; no replay is mandatory.

The following are historical manual-rule candidate conditions. [The mastery contract](design/STORY_MASTERY_CONTRACT.md) defines current-pattern goals and versioned records; real Story feasibility and runtime qualification remain prerequisites before ratings presentation. Each proposed goal earns at most one star; one action may satisfy both.

| Location | Accomplishment A | Accomplishment B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lantern Landing | **Collect a grown star.** A committed clear includes a value-two star. | **Collect 6,000 base points at once.** One clear collects at least six underlying light units; an ordinary tap qualifies. Earned bonuses cannot substitute for this ingredient requirement. |
| Ribbon Crossing | **Make a useful exchange.** An exchange creates a legal match absent before it, and the next board-changing action clears it using a moved star. | **Connect separated stars.** A valid trace clears at least five distinct stars spanning at least two pre-clear orthogonal matching components. |
| Kite Harbor | **Open a crowded column.** One clear reduces a currently tallest column, at least four cells high, by at least two occupied cells. Ties qualify. | **Keep a match ready for after the shower.** Preserve a ready group while clearing a different taller column; let one shower arrive, then collect the banked ingredients with the next clear. No intervening exchange or second shower. |
| Seed Moon | **Harvest four grown stars.** Collect four distinct value-two stars during the successful run. | **Save a grown star for the next clear.** A selected clear leaves a grown member of its larger matching component, and the next clear collects that survivor. No intervening swap or shower. |
| Silver Pools | **Create a relay.** A clear's settling creates a previously absent recipe; the next clear spends exactly that recipe's ingredients. | **Set up two follow-ups.** One clear creates two previously absent, disjoint legal matches; collect both within the next two clears, with no intervening swap or shower. An efficient single clear collecting both also qualifies. |

For causal objectives, compare committed before/after states and actual ingredient identities. A matching group or recipe must genuinely be created, not merely still exist after an unrelated action. The Silver double-setup detector must prove that the second group remains collectable after the first follow-up; identify a valid authored example before building UI around it.

Kite's supply should naturally present its crowded columns; players must not need to manufacture danger for a star. Its banked group may gain stars or grow during the allowed shower, provided all banked identities survive and are collected. Every location's two accomplishments must be achievable naturally before the target without deliberately farming extra showers.

Pause, reading, menu visits, backgrounding and restoring a save are not board actions and do not break a pending achievement. A committed arrival or exchange can change the relevant setup and legitimately end its causal opportunity. Equivalent accessible actions qualify; gesture precision and input method never change the award.

These stars recognize demonstrated understanding, not optimal play. Unlimited exchanges can construct useful arrangements; experimentation is legitimate. Avoid requirements such as no pause, fewest gestures, never making a mistake, maintaining a perfect height throughout the run, or collecting a quota of disconnected recipes.

Score-only completion tiers remain a poor fit for an immediately terminal score target: grading final overshoot would reward the last group's size disproportionately. A future distinct score-attack mode could use performance thresholds on a fixed opportunity budget. For this restoration proposal, accomplishments remain optional and separate from the earned-score investigation. Global move or shower pars would introduce new incentives against experimentation or in favor of extensive paused rearrangement.

Store the best rating from a single completed run. Do not combine A from one run with B from another into a three-star rating. A weaker replay never removes earned stars. If human play finds the simultaneous goals burdensome, the bounded alternative is independently persistent accomplishment stamps; decide that explicitly rather than mixing the two semantics.

## Keep shower timing, omit a stage countdown

Automatic showers already create a rhythm and spatial pressure. Keep the existing countdown to the next shower, explicit pause, clear-while-paused and manual advance. Overflow remains the loss condition.

Add no whole-stage time limit, speed bonus, time-based star, automatic speed ramp or rating penalty for reading or accessibility use. A player can finish at their own pace with the same stars and restoration. Preserve existing interruption grace and no catch-up showers.

Do not add a minimum-time completion lock. Use authored content and measured supply to establish a typical length. Treat unexpectedly fast new-player clears as a pacing observation to investigate; skilled fast replays are a success.

A separate optional time challenge is compatible with the Puzzle League reference: its Time Zone mode has a score deadline, while other modes have different win conditions. Such a mode is outside the current restoration work; its clock should serve a clear score-attack purpose.

## Protect progress and make rating evidence authoritative

Historical [challenge targets](../Sources/StarfallCore/StarfallChallenge.swift) are computed from identity. The first slice now makes [FallingSky](../Sources/StarfallCore/FallingSky.swift) resolve the objective through the saved content revision when deriving outcomes and validating saves. Changing the historical global target alone could reopen an archived win or invalidate a retained seed/balance contract.

The first slice saves and validates the challenge's content revision and target/supply/scoring contract. Missing revision means the historical 18-light definition, not the newest content. Resume and same-round replay retain that definition. An explicit new visit starts the new revision; preview/cancel preserves the saved round. Historical scores continue to display their original 18-light scale. Future ratings need separately validated completed goal flags and bounded pending causal evidence.

Existing repaired places and access stay earned. Historical verified wins get completion credit only; do not invent earlier mastery. Prior-install access without a recorded win remains access, not a star. Keep best results bound to their content/objective revision so later incompatible rebalancing does not fabricate evidence or remove historical achievements.

Core owns all qualifications and the final award. Evaluate the target-crossing action's achievements before finalizing the rating. Persist the committed outcome before animation. The current App relay feedback is transient and cannot serve as a saved award authority. World progress writes must remain idempotent and recoverable from a validated winning round.

## Acceptance and calibration

- [ ] Every stage has a reproducible completion path, two differing meaningful choices and recoverable alternatives. Check paths beyond one solved seed/script, including neighboring action orders and a missed opportunity.
- [ ] Lantern remains approachable through its actual saved pattern rules. A player's first repair does not depend on optional mastery, a reflex deadline or a perfect input sequence.
- [ ] Each accomplishment has at least one verified earning path and rejection cases for superficially similar but unrelated actions. Check duplicate events, the winning clear and interruption/restore between causal steps.
- [ ] Human observation separates discovery from coached reproduction. Ask what changed between locations, why a particular clear helped and whether the player wants another location or optional replay.
- [ ] Observe several first-time players, starting with a small formative group of roughly five. Record confusion, idle stretches, recovery and active-play duration manually; no analytics or identifying data. This is a revision aid, not a statistical enjoyment claim.
- [ ] Calibrate targets after the authored supplies. Compare simple matching, stronger setup policies and actual native play; never derive human timing or a difficulty rating from solver speed.
- [ ] Validate theatrical score layouts and effect tiers with exact large values, rapid clears and quiet modes. Contrast ordinary awards with exceptional play without covering survivors or delaying input.
- [ ] Record first-clear and practiced-replay duration separately. No win is withheld to satisfy a minimum time; content arithmetic never counts unbuilt rounds or future multiplayer as delivered hours.
- [ ] Follow the [verification matrix](ENGINEERING.md#verification-by-change): deterministic Core/rating/save tests, affected hosted/UI checks, Debug/Release builds and membership, hygiene and whitespace. Exercise pause/manual, replay/new visit, old wins, background/restore, quiet/normal motion and largest text. Actual VoiceOver and physical feel remain distinct evidence.
- [ ] Keep source-bound summaries and exact results. Adopt or remove candidates; do not retain unexplained flags, alternative targets or obsolete copy.
