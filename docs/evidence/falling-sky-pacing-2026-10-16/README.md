# Falling-sky pacing and feedback — 2026-10-16

New skies open with three adjacent matches. Subsequent colored stars share a shower color. When a board has no legal group, the next generated preview reserves three matching stars for a lowest column with at least three empty cells, plus two pressure arrivals. This is bounded forecast generation, not a board rewrite. Announced previews remain unchanged. Dark-star frequency and overflow remain active. Old saves missing `cohesiveShowers` keep the previous generation algorithm; replay/new sky uses the current rules.

The same 200 seeds and 22-shower, one-clear-per-shower policy are recorded in `pacing.json`. Baseline first match averaged 7.28 showers; some had no match within the sample. Candidate first matches were all in shower 1; mean clears increased from 4.04 to 12.67, with a minimum of 7. Sampled idle skies all overflowed by shower 25. This does not establish human difficulty, fun, or actual 90-second input timing.

Comet travel is 0.48s instead of 0.72s, with 0.09s staggering instead of 0.13s. The steady first shower is scheduled at four seconds and its presentation lasts about 0.94s. The clock pace itself remains selectable at 3/4/6s. Call next shower resets the live deadline or advances once while paused; paused clears are allowed. Busy, background and terminal input remain gated.

Clear rings and a short score popup are bounded and removed at settlement. Ready groups have static outlines and a count, also present with reduced motion. Optional sound reuses QuietTuckPlayer; optional soft haptics use GameFeedback. Both default off and persist locally. Muting, pause, restart and suspension stop owned sound; VoiceOver announcements avoid competing sound.
