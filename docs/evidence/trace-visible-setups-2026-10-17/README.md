**Result:** Partially verified · **Owner:** current implementer · **Date:** 2026-10-17

## Decision and remaining uncertainty

Retain the analysis for source-bound reproduction. Stop searching this small sample for a persuasive holding example: none has earned a native comparison. The whole-versus-five strategy has failed to demonstrate an immediate payoff here. This narrows the next experiment to visible opportunity cost, not more shower tuning or a larger offline search presented as enjoyment.

A white connector is only a hypothesis. Its earlier ordinary-only trial showed understandable consumption, but neither a user-tested hold benefit nor selected-trace compatibility. Require a visible before-shower benefit with all competing ordinary taps and full traces examined; otherwise revise/remove that adaptation. Keep production rules unchanged and one phone lab.
