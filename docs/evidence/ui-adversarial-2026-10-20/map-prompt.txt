Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one high-fidelity portrait iPhone game map screen concept for Starfall, full-bleed illustrated world with visible destination markers and one selected-place card. This is a design proposal, not a production screenshot.
Reference 1: existing WorldMapRestored painting. Preserve the identities, richness, navy/purple cloud atmosphere, craft, and connected floating-world feel. Do not copy its poster layout, baked giant logo or slogan.
Reference 2: approved Observatory UI concept. Match its midnight enamel, restrained fine brass fittings, pearl serif headings and clean readable labels. Do not reproduce the triptych.
Composition: a single tall portrait screen about 9:19, no external phone bezel. The illustrated world fills all edges behind the UI and feels like a place to explore. A compact safe-top native-like header says "Story", with a back chevron at left and a labeled "Places" utility at right. A small line below reads "Choose a place to restore". Camera is high three-quarter overhead with coherent supports, no horizon. Show a winding journey of five clearly recognizable substantial locations, from Lantern Landing lower-left through Ribbon Crossing mid-left, Kite Harbor mid-right, Seed Moon upper-right and Silver Pools upper-left. Bridges and paths connect them. The five locations are larger than in the old twelve-node poster. Future world silhouettes recede gently into clouds at the distant top without competing with actionable locations.
Illustrated example progress: Lantern Landing and Ribbon Crossing are restored and have compact check-mark plaques with readable names. Kite Harbor is selected and ready, with a clearly visible brass pin, restrained single selection halo, name plaque and "Ready" label. Seed Moon and Silver Pools each have a small readable lock marker and name label. Their shapes remain legible under soft mist. Distinguish states by symbols and text, not color alone. No giant numbered badges. Controls look physically crafted but have simple silhouettes.
One compact selected-location instrument at the bottom safe edge occupies no more than the lower fifth: title "Kite Harbor", subtitle "Ready to restore", one wide action "Visit Kite Harbor". Make every text line readable at phone size; keep artwork and key landmarks above this card. No second menu stack, no repeated status cards, no giant logo, no slogan, no in-game grid or score/currency, no XP, shop, purchase, energy, lives or backend UI.
Mood: magical living illustrated game, with subtle clouds, a few small bright route glints and softly lit lanterns suggesting gentle ambient motion; do not show exaggerated motion streaks or pulsing all nodes. Real motion is not proved by this still. Keep precise type, sparse hierarchy and substantial connected terrain.
