# Current guidance and source-bound history

Start from the [documentation map](README.md) and [writing process](WRITING.md). Use its linked plan/design/report/PR/issue templates, scale to the task and remove unused prompts. Lead with the outcome or recommendation, important limits and next action. Supporting detail may be linked or collapsed; blockers, failed checks and material risks must remain visible.

- Keep README/ROADMAP and the current experiment/engineering indexes consistent with code and adopted evidence. Label historical research explicitly; preserve its original source and results. Approved future designs do not establish implemented behavior. Do not rewrite old evidence merely to match a new format.
- Ordinary maintained folders need a short README under the [folder protocol](WRITING.md#give-folders-a-useful-front-door). Packaged, sealed, historical-payload and generated folders inherit their documented owner. Do not modify their contents/hashes for documentation coverage.
- Use Mermaid or captioned images when they clarify a decision, labeling concepts separately from runtime evidence. For visual product docs, inspect and embed relevant existing assets from the [visual reference](design/VISUAL_REFERENCE.md), with alt text and source/status captions. Text-only tooling docs need no decoration.
- Follow [screenshot practice](SCREENSHOTS.md): begin with the selected capture index; mark affected entries Current, Needs refresh or Historical. Retained captures identify actual version/build or commit, dirty-input snapshot, screen, device and variant, never latest/final/current filenames. Retire superseded captures in small recoverable batches, preserving unique unresolved evidence and sealed payloads.
- Reports identify tested source, dirty inputs, commands, configuration, environment and result. Separate core/build, interaction, inspected visual, observed animation, actual VoiceOver and physical-device evidence. Mark missing or inapplicable acceptance plainly. Put raw output in ignored artifacts/temp storage and small durable summaries in `evidence`.
- Maintain the [experiment register](EXPERIMENTS.md) at adoption/extension/removal decisions. Deferred concrete risks belong in [ENGINEERING](ENGINEERING.md), with a trigger and verification. Link authoritative procedures instead of duplicating them. Documentation adds no approval gate.
