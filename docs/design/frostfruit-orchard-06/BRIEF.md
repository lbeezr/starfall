## A gathering place that happens to be wintry

Stars tend the branches, collect drifting luminous seeds and pause beneath the tree to listen. Murk's passage left its fruit opaque and its veins dormant; the path, winter landscape and evidence of care remain intact. At completion, the last sealed cluster opens into clearly separated glass lobes around a luminous center, with a few seeds beginning their drift toward the onward route. This must read in a still without a character performance or sound.

The final difference is an opening, not a brighter copy. Preserve trunk, branch supports, path, terrace edges, camera, scale and backdrop registration while changing fruit opacity, veins and the authored cluster. Seed silhouettes are small tapered organic forms, never miniature playable stars or incoming shower dots. Their presence belongs to the static final painting; continuous fruit sway, a chime performance and seed simulation are not part of this batch.
