| Model | Production demand | Progress/save consequence | Recommendation |

| **One repair per place** | Twelve distinct encounters and payoffs; five new locations remain. | Fits existing location-completion semantics. Define each new challenge/version explicitly. | **Baseline.** Complete places and make decisions distinct. |
| **One optional variation at one place** | One different opening/development/capstone, with a recovery route; reuse paintings where suitable. | Needs stable round/content identity, selection and replay semantics. Existing repair/unlock remains earned. | Bounded later comparison if a second decision is valuable. |
| **Three/four rounds throughout** | 36/48 authored encounters are scope arithmetic, not committed output or measured duration. | Needs versioned partial round progress and explicit location-completion rules; current `WorldJourney` cannot represent that model alone. | Do not roll out by default. Compare the one-place pilot first. |

Preserve existing completed repairs, selected place and original-five legacy access; world progress and validated round archives remain separate owners. Continue/same-round Replay keep objective, rules, supply, RNG and previews. Preview/cancel preserves the active round until Begin. Practice never earns repairs. Optional mastery [#253](https://github.com/lbeezr/starfall/issues/253), extra rounds and mandatory progression are separate decisions.

No minimum-duration lock, mandatory waiting or promised hours follows from this budget. Reusing scenery reduces some painting demand; it does not eliminate encounter, state, replay or verification work. Asset size, memory, frame pacing and sustained-device budgets remain measured work in [#207](https://github.com/lbeezr/starfall/issues/207), not guessed numbers here.

Nearby versus and cooperative restoration remain named future directions in the [reference guide](POKEMON_PUZZLE_LEAGUE_REFERENCE.md#future-local-play). This production plan keeps today's offline scope; it adds no networking, account, backend or shared-progress implementation.

## Bell Choir: restore a public music place

Three unequal golden-glass chimes hang from a substantial copper arch above a public terrace. Rounded fittings, a waiting ledge, an approach path and an onward connection make a connected place; the clappers are crafted objects distinct from the living cast. Preserve the rich terrain/depth of Lantern and Ribbon, with warm glass, visible joins and broad local light paths rather than uniform ornament.

**Proposed player question:** Take a useful short pattern to open the crowded side now, or preserve its still-incomplete ingredients while arranging a different clear whose falling survivors could create another phase. Both choices must help the announced restoration; the chain is optional. A complete automatic pattern cannot wait to be tapped later.

This is not a solved encounter. Its future runtime packet must provide one exact before-state, two legal action witnesses through the selected production resolver, different survivors/headroom/points, a fixed announced supply continuation and a recoverable alternative. Use visible heights and materials; do not grade hidden receiving-column knowledge. Select and version opening, supply, objective and cadence deliberately. The template's 18-light thresholds are not Bell's target. Resolve the current four-line trial's implications when adopting its rule family; installation alone proves no balance preference.

No pitch identification, beat timing, three-key gate, new star type, power-up or required final chain is part of this brief. The player earns repair through the ordinary committed gameplay objective; music reinforces it.
