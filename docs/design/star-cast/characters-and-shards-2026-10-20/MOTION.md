# Gameplay shard production pass

**Implemented and development-verified, October 20, 2026.** The approved seven faceless pieces become the normal game's forecast, falling and settled materials. Original face-bearing characters remain the story cast. This pass changes presentation, not matching, scores, showers, saved state or progression.

[Native source, captures and verification](../../../evidence/gameplay-shards-2026-10-20/README.md) record development acceptance and physical/RC limits.

## Acceptance

- All seven approved contours and color families survive transparent packaging and native small-size rendering, including round white Opal and the admired dark folded Dusk.
- Forecast, airborne arrival and resting piece use the same material, pivot and family identity. A landing hands off one body without an extra flash or duplicate.
- Each family's fall/contact/nearby-event response feels appropriate to its mass and outline. Motion is finite, settles exactly, and cannot change cell identity, hit geometry or committed outcomes.
- Restart, replacement and quiet-motion settings cancel active transforms and restore neutral artwork. Full normal animation is inspected separately from stills and quiet checks.
- Grown pieces have one enlarged body with a separate cached aura. No offset body copies, paired glints or baked halo. The existing ordinary body is roughly 0.69 cell spacing wide; literal double width would overlap adjacent pieces. Keep normal readability and choose a bounded enlarged body after measuring the dense board, recording its actual factor.
- Native tests, Debug/Release resource builds and inspected board captures establish development acceptance. Physical feel and actual VoiceOver remain separate evidence.

## Motion direction

| Family | Fall | Contact and nearby impulse | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spark | Lead with the round comet mass; bank into travel | Quick roll away, short counter-bank | Approved diagonal comet contour |
| Sunny | Low, weighty turning | Short, firm rebound | Unequal broad lobes |
| Pip | A small angular tumble | Rock onto its broad base with little bounce | Flat-sided wedge |
| Twinkle | Small hanging-glint sway | Narrow pendulum response | Tall diamond |
| Nova | Shallow lens banking | Broad, low-amplitude yaw and pitch | Wide lens |
| Dusk | Slow turn of the folded mass | Restrained counter-rock | Original folded silhouette |
| Opal | Existing soft teardrop stretch | Soft squash, rebound and recovery | Circular pearl |

Roll is rotation in the screen plane. Yaw and pitch are shallow presentation deformations; these 2D sprites do not contain unseen back faces. Limit the turn before a family loses its identifying outline. No family needs continuous idle wobble.

Crystal highlights and colored bevels belong in the cutout. Auras, brief contact light and growth emphasis belong in the renderer, where they can respond to motion preferences and events. No floor shadow is added to floating pieces.
