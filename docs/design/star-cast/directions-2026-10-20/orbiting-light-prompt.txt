DIRECTION: Each character has a compact, luminous, SOLID central body carrying the face. Separate broad geometric RAY SEGMENTS hover very close around this core with clearly intentional narrow air gaps, together completing an unmistakably star-shaped silhouette. The floating rays are the character's living anatomy. They tilt, gather, brace and open as the character pays attention and makes choices. They are NOT extra hands, accessories, jewelry, orbit rings, crystals on sticks, or a molecule diagram. Each ray is one broad solid shape with soft beveled facets; use a few large planes, not shattered fragments. Keep every ray close enough that the whole reads as one creature, not a swarm. Maintain the ray count and family recognition throughout the sheet.

MATERIAL: Warm painterly dimensional starlight held in tactile translucent stone. Broad softly rounded planes, warm upper-left illumination, gently shaded lower-right depth. Quiet face plane with color retained. Local luminosity inside the body, no aura, no bloom cloud, no duplicate outline. The design should feel alive and neighborly, never a futuristic robot, metal drone, hologram or abstract icon.

SUNNY: Amber/gold, FIVE separated broad rays around a warm compact horizontally generous core. Broad, open, welcoming silhouette; an expansive fan-like opening gesture. A distinctive WIDE low smile and small friendly crescent-like eyelids with visible directed pupils, not huge glossy infant eyes. Face reads mature warmth and reassurance, a good host who notices somebody else before the broken object. Make the eye spacing, lid construction and mouth much different from Pip and Twinkle. Top proposal leans warmly toward Twinkle, rays gently opening in welcome; no limbs.

PIP: Mint, SIX separated rays. Compact angular core and crisp, braced, deliberate ray arrangement. Precise asymmetry in pivots and stance, as though measuring how something fits. CLOSE-SPACED focused SLANTED eyes with small directed pupils under decisive brows; tiny off-center firm mouth. No baby eyes, no generic grin. Top proposal angles downward with one lower ray precisely pointing toward a small seam in the ground; a clear practical inspection pose, no hands or tool. Confident competence rather than anger.

TWINKLE: Rose, EIGHT alternating long/short slender broad-plane rays, softer narrower central core, finer overall proportions than Sunny and Pip. Her rays gather close like a shy fan on one side and open subtly toward a source of returning light. A distinctive attentive SIDE GAZE, longer soft upper eyelids and a very SMALL restrained mouth; eyes neither Pip's slants nor Sunny's crescents. Gentle attention, not sleepiness or generic cuteness. Top proposal notices a tiny warm reflection and leans toward it while keeping an inward protective shape; no limbs.

FACIAL STUDIES: Render only the central face/core in graphite, without colored rays or glow, making the three different facial structures obvious. Two facial states beneath each corresponding character: neutral, concerned. Concern must change lids, gaze, tension and mouth in character-specific ways, not put the identical sad emoji on all three.

SILENT STORY — four bottom panels, consistent continuous warm painterly setting and same three characters:
Panel 1: A small old brass lantern has gone dark. Sunny notices Twinkle's disappointment; Pip is already looking toward the lantern's base seam; Twinkle's rose rays draw close. Every character attends to something specific.
Panel 2: Sunny turns toward and comforts Twinkle by leaning close and opening a protective fan of rays, while Pip leans down to inspect the lantern's base with a precisely aimed ray. Three different choices, no hands, speech balloons or captions.
Panel 3: A first tiny light sputters out. Sunny's encouraging smile catches in surprise, Pip's angled brows and tightly braced rays show frustrated concentration, Twinkle recoils slightly and looks toward Sunny, rose rays gathering. Do not give them matching expressions.
Panel 4: They willingly share their light together, shown by three short soft warm colored light ribbons from their intact central bodies converging gently into the now warmly relit lantern. Sunny opens with relief, Pip notices the successful fit with a small satisfied look, Twinkle attends to the returning reflection with a restrained smile. All three can share light; this is cooperation, not three different powers. All bodies/rays remain intact.
