Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: silhouette-first character design exploration for Starfall, an original small celestial puzzle game. Landscape 1536x1024 concept board.
Primary request: Six GENUINELY DIFFERENT interpretations of living starlight in a shared expressive crystal material. The earlier designs failed because every body was the same radial star with more points. Abandon that construction. This sheet must show six distinct black masses before facial expressions or color can help. The only shape constraint is that each works as a compact readable repeated game piece.
Reference image 1: previous Expressive Crystal sheet. Use ONLY its luminous crystal material, painterly dimensional quality, expressive lids, off-white sclera, directed dark pupils and integrated mouths. Do NOT copy any star outline, number of rays, proportions, composition or pose from the reference.
Shared construction: substantial central mass with space for a readable face; one connected body; broad forms and purposeful asymmetry. Keep every body comfortably inside its own compact tile, with longest axis around 70 percent of tile width. Aspect ratios roughly 1 to 1.5, equal apparent visual weight. No long trailing tails, wispy outlines, orbiting fragments, tiny decorative appendages, hands, feet, costumes, badges or little chest lights. No common five-point star anywhere in the sheet. Very restrained broad crystal planes, dark colored bevel/shadow and soft inner light; avoid dense triangular facet noise and large external glow.
SPARK — SHOOTING LIGHT: aqua, a compact lively swept comet shape. A large full rounded leading head at lower-right flows into ONE fused upswept triangular shoulder/tail at upper-left, strongly asymmetric teardrop silhouette. The tail is short, thick and curved back over the body, entirely within the tile. This is a solid character body, not a round face followed by a detached trail. Open alert curious eyes; body leans eagerly toward what it notices.
SUNNY — LITTLE SUN: amber/gold, broad squat full solar body, roughly rounded-square or soft irregular hexagon in overall mass. Its edges have a few shallow broad organic corona bulges with NO pointed star rays. More broad than tall, full low center, almost cushion-like luminous solar volume. A wide welcoming asymmetrical smile and smaller warm eyes. Distinct from both a plain circular orb and a radial five-point star. Do not add an external ring or ray halo to create the sun shape.
PIP — FALLEN STAR: mint, compact asymmetric faceted meteor nugget, an irregular beveled trapezoid with one noticeably slanted flat top and a broad sturdy base. No projecting rays, tail or crystal cluster. Thick useful corners, substantial body mass; a small slanted facet could nudge something with precision. A focused close-spaced gaze and slightly wry mouth, practical and interested rather than angry.
TWINKLE — GLINT: rose, a compact pinched-waist diffraction cross, like the flash of a single twinkling light. Exactly one continuous broad diamond center with a short long-axis extension above and below and two much shorter lateral triangular shoulders. Strongly convex center contrasted with deep curved pinches toward the four ends. Tall rather than wide, but at most 1.5:1, with enough face area. NO six/eight-point radial body, no many small rays, no thin needles. Attentive sideways eyes and quiet lifted smile; no eyelashes as identity shorthand.
NOVA — DISTANT FLARE: violet, low and wide smooth lenticular crystal body, like a distant light flattened on the horizon. Two broad lateral tips with continuous bowed upper and lower contours, NO top point or bottom point. A full lens-shaped central mass, width at most 1.5x height, substantial enough to hold the face. Low relaxed eyes and a thoughtful half-smile. Not a flying saucer, not a ring, no mechanical seams.
DUSK — FOLDED LIGHT: charcoal-indigo with a restrained lilac edge, a compact chunky folded ray. Two unequal rounded wedge-like lobes connected by a wide diagonal central bridge. One deep rounded notch enters from upper-left and an opposing broad notch enters from lower-right, giving a unique folded flowing contour and a solid center for the face. Blunt ends, organic weight, no radial star, no moon/crescent, horns, spiky devil shape, disconnected parts, lightning-bolt tip, literal letter Z or portal. A playful sideways glance, raised lid and friendly crooked smile. Distinct from Spark's single sweeping teardrop.
Layout: clean warm ivory concept board, no scenic story panels. Title "STARFALL · DIFFERENT KINDS OF STARLIGHT", small subtitle "Expressive Crystal · silhouette exploration". Six evenly spaced columns. Top row: six large fully rendered characters in the order Spark, Sunny, Pip, Twinkle, Nova, Dusk. Their exact names and short interpretation labels beneath each. Middle row: six SOLID BLACK SILHOUETTES of those EXACT bodies at matching scale and pose, NO eyes, mouths, facets, shading, white facial marks or glow. Only the outline should distinguish them. These black copies must accurately match the rendered designs above. Make enough empty space between copies.
Bottom row: a compact sample grid on dark blue-gray, 6 columns by 3 rows, mixing and repeating the six same character designs as SMALL pieces within faint square cells. All pieces wholly inside their cells, no overlap, no trails. Keep each face near its cell center and all six forms recognizable. This is only an illustrative density comparison; caption "Illustrative board spacing · not a gameplay capture". Do not depict the original radial star outlines in the grid.
Quality priority: silhouette differentiation first, character appeal second, polished shared material third. Do not satisfy the request by giving the same basic star different point counts. Sunny must be broad solar mass, Pip a compact flat-sided chunk, Spark a single compact comet, Twinkle a tall pinched glint, Nova a wide unpointed lens, Dusk a chunky concave fold. Their black silhouettes must remain different without color or labels. No paired internal light marks, no grown state, no extra stars, no extra cast members.
