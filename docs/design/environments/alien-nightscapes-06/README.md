# The same travelers, an enormous universe

**Status:** Research and proposed direction · **Owner:** current implementer · **Updated:** October 18, 2026

Give Starfall a coherent journey through distinct nightscapes. Keep the recognizable crystal cast, tender expressions and warm starlight; change the scale, silhouettes and celestial phenomena surrounding them. **Moonwake and Nebula Cradle remain the starting points.** The other three destinations below are hypotheses, not generated or implemented scenes.

**Recommendation:** Each destination needs one unmistakable physical feature and one small sign of life or travel. “The stars are traveling” supplies continuity without adding a campaign, progression or unlocks. The centered gameplay stays independent of the surroundings.

**Next action:** Review the [five scene kits and storyboards](../scene-kits-07/README.md) before full generation. They define layer roles, separate props, portrait placements and environmental beats. A broken-world passage remains a useful later test of gentle alien identity.

## What the WoW references teach us

The recent ethereal setting is **K’aresh**, the shattered homeworld featured in The War Within. Blizzard describes fragments of a former living world and surviving eco-domes. Its official images juxtapose a large violet vortex, angular silhouettes and enclosed greenery. The lesson for us is a visible environmental premise, plus a small place of refuge. [Blizzard’s K’aresh zone article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24223014/the-war-within-the-zone-and-dungeon-of-karesh).

| Reference | Observation from the sampled imagery | Starfall translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Netherstorm — Burning Crusade** | Luminous rounded domes stand apart from jagged dark terrain. Strong silhouette contrast makes the location recognizable before details resolve. | A distant suspended habitat, seen from open air. Keep its glow subdued and off the gameplay field. |
| **Nagrand — Burning Crusade** | Floating islands and falling water establish vertical separation. Land has mass while the gaps read as navigable air. The sampled image is daylight. | Use the depth cue at night: a few islands far below or at the margins. No island becomes a shelf beneath the stars. |
| **Argus — Legion** | A huge blue planetary edge dwarfs nearby figures and architecture. Its partial silhouette implies more world outside the frame. | A cropped moon or broken planetary arc can identify a place without surrounding the field with scenery. |
| **Shadowmoon Valley — Warlords of Draenor** | The sampled grove uses deep blue foliage, soft atmospheric separation and isolated warm lights. Blizzard explicitly describes perpetual night. This is the Draenor version, not Burning Crusade’s fel landscape. | A planetary night with recognizable alien life, viewed from above its canopy. Leave the central air dark and clear. |
| **K’aresh — The War Within** | The vortex, fragments and enclosed habitats establish a specific broken-world setting. Brightness is concentrated in a few landmarks. | A passage through remnants of a planet. Preserve wonder and refuge; soften the harsh silhouettes and keep the main phenomenon away from the stars. |

These are our visual interpretations of a bounded reference sample, not an audit of every zone. Sources and the exact inspected image URLs are in the [visual study](index.html). Additional primary sources: [Nagrand Skylands](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20050250/the-six-best-romantic-picnic-spots), [Shadowmoon zone preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/14061289/warlords-of-draenor-zone-preview-shadowmoon-valley), [Argus campaign preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20979713/patch-73-preview-the-campaign-for-argus-updated-sept-12), [Burning Crusade deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23625673/world-of-warcraft-explorando-a-fundo-burning-crusade-classic). ## How the sky and foreground belong together

Argus artist Phillip Zhang describes bringing hand-painted matte imagery into WoW skyboxes, blending it with playable terrain, and designing sky sections to correspond with foreground points of interest. His portfolio includes panoramas and animation demonstrations. We read the artist’s descriptions; this pass did not play through the zones or analyze the video timing. [Mac’Aree skybox](https://phillipzhang.artstation.com/projects/5go9J), [Krokuun skybox](https://phillipzhang.artstation.com/projects/oqlYz).

For Starfall, use that compositional relationship in a smaller five-plane assembly: distant space; a defining celestial body or phenomenon; far silhouettes; nearer edge forms; sparse foreground dust or wisps. A moon’s lit edge should illuminate nearby dust consistently. A fragment arc should share perspective with its distant debris. Every layer needs a reason to exist beyond filling the frame. This is our proposed 2.5D approach, not a claim about Blizzard’s exact asset or renderer structure.

Landscape and portrait need authored placements of the same parts. Scaling/cropping one painting cannot reliably retain the landmark, protect the HUD and preserve the centered field at once. The [Nebula layer proof](../open-skies-05/nebula-layers/README.md) explores this with browser compositing only.

## Five possible stops on the journey

| Destination | Defining shape and experience | Palette and restrained motion | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Moonwake** | Passing close to a pearl moon; one enormous curve, a tiny distant sail | Midnight blue, pearl, restrained gold; slow dust drift | Existing concept; moon/sail extraction still needed |
| **The Nebula Cradle** | A sheltered pocket in star-forming gas; billowing forms imply immense depth | Indigo, dusty violet and rose; separate distant haze and nearer cloud movement | Five-layer browser proof |
| **Shatterlight Reach** | Open space through a broken planet’s widely separated remnants; a tiny surviving light suggests somewhere worth visiting | Ink blue, muted lavender rock, small amber lights; very slow debris parallax | Proposed new setting |
| **The Night Garden** | Traveling above an alien nocturnal canopy; luminous seedpods and branching silhouettes far below | Midnight teal, blue-violet, tiny mint lights; sparse ascending spores | Proposed planetary setting |
| **Ringdrift** | Crossing a vast ringed planet’s shadow; two or three broad arcs imply distance | Blue-black, pale icy cyan, a narrow rose rim; slow edge glints | Proposed deep-space setting |

The common identity comes first from the characters, expressive material and art direction. A small recurring celestial sail or wayfinding beacon is an optional continuity cue, not a required mascot, collectible or button. Keep astronomical instruments as distant artifacts of this universe when they help a place; avoid rebuilding a supporting observatory platform.

## Acceptance before another roster is adopted

- Distinguish destinations by major shapes in a small grayscale view, not only by hue. Test planetary and open-space scenes together.
- Keep all settings night; the cast is the most immediate bright, colorful subject. Environment detail must remain distinguishable from incoming or playable stars.
- Retain each destination’s defining feature in both iPad orientations and phone portrait. Preserve the centered 7×7 mapping and clear HUD/action areas.
- Permit bounded scenic motion only; hold all game geometry still and stop environmental motion with Reduce Motion. Native hit mapping, performance and interruption checks remain required before adoption.
- Compare with the existing cast and dense/empty fields. No lore UI, journey map, campaign, progression or unlocks are part of this work.

**Limits:** Screenshot/article research, not gameplay observation or engine reverse engineering. Public reference media remains at its original sources; none is vendored or packaged in Starfall. No new scene roster has been generated or adopted from this research.
