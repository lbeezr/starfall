## Art-direction correction · October 19

The user identified a drift toward simpler scenes after Seed Moon and named Lantern Landing and Ribbon Crossing as the stronger standard. The first Silver Pools masters overconstrained landmark clearance and reduced the garden to an isolated fountain. Those attempts remain in provenance but are rejected as the shipping direction. The revised family uses the first two shipped locations as framing references: broad terraced garden, offscreen paths, resting ledges, planted beds, drainage details and cropped foreground stonework. The arch and three main bowls remain the recognizable restoration story. A quiet central sky is composed naturally inside that place; it is not cleared, masked or used as a reason to remove the surrounding world.
