Do the choices reach the player?

Watch an ordinary round in the single Starfall app. Keep this worksheet out of the player’s view. Fill it after observing; unknown is a valid answer. Nothing is uploaded or saved automatically.

Before explaining anything

  1. Let the player choose a new sky. Record a retained sky or prepared board as such.
  2. Watch the first useful clear, storm and Opal encounter. Do not point out a solution.
  3. If you ask “What do you expect that clear to change?”, record the answer before the action. Mark a neutral prompt or strategy help accurately. Pause if needed and record mixed/paused play.
  4. Afterward ask what changed their decision and why. If they had time, observe whether they actually start another sky.

A missed choice can mean it was absent, hard to see or blocked by input. Keep those explanations separate. Use anonymous P/S IDs; keep names and contact details out of records.
