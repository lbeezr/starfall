## Parked: supernova candidates

These are isolated rule experiments, not app features or adopted power-ups. No new runtime flag, save field, asset or dependency is added. The runner copies the current core into a temporary folder and applies a strict patch there; mismatched patches fail rather than silently using different rules.

Run `python3 tools/mechanics/run-supernova.py` for matching-identity recoloring, or add `--rotate` for cyclic identity rotation. Both execute fixture regressions and 200-seed samples before emitting JSON. The candidate threshold is five starlight; ordinary clears retain the three-starlight threshold. Recolor only orthogonal surviving colored neighbors of the original group before settling. Preserve survivor IDs and growth, ordinary Dusk removal, score conservation, preview and RNG. Never cascade. Rotation follows the existing `Jewel.allCases` order and is a diagnostic rule, not an approved character transformation order.

Policies clear every available group, choosing the first canonical group. They compare ordinary tap, charge whenever eligible, and charge only when it creates more immediately available groups. This last policy examines committed hypothetical states; it does not simulate a person's comprehension, finger timing, preview or charge gesture. Counts across policies have different visited states and denominators. `differingFollowups` compares settled boards; better/worse counts compare legal-group counts, not eventual survival or enjoyment. Equal group counts can conceal strategically different boards. Each committed state is checked for valid-save invariants; every clear is round-tripped and its next shower compared after restore.

The depth metrics also compare total immediately clearable starlight and overflow through the already announced shower with no intervening clear. `tapExamples` retains up to three exact pre-input states from the ordinary-tap trajectory where tapping preserves more clearable value or avoids immediate overflow. These are replay fixtures, not normal app saves. Future unseen previews may differ between branches. Zero observed overflow differences is not a proof of equivalence or lack of strategic depth.

Decision point: do not promote either version yet. The October 17 sample shows stronger immediate follow-ups overwhelmingly favor charging; neither establishes a sufficient reason to prefer a tap on eligible groups. Resolve the tradeoff and preview/comprehension design before adoption. The interaction lab below is parked following the user's shower-variety choice. Do not infer that a higher bot score means greater enjoyment. ## Isolated native interaction lab

Normal tap stays available. Groups worth five or more support a 0.65-second hold followed by release; dragging outside the original cell after arming cancels permanently for that touch. A shower, restart, pause, settings/background suspension, balance/motion change or scene settlement cancels the intent. Each touch carries a ticket so older callbacks cannot operate on a newer finger's intent.
