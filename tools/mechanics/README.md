## Pacing comparison

`python3 tools/mechanics/compare-pacing.py --seeds 16 --seconds 120` compiles the unchanged Debug Store and real trace intent on macOS, with a virtual clock and test-only scene/feedback substitutes. It uses the adopted Core cadence through a thin compatibility adapter in `Tests/StarfallCoreTests/TimingCycleFixture.swift`; its A lookup remains a tool-only historical comparison. No alternate algorithm or app selector is added. Requires Xcode command-line tools. The runner never installs or launches an app.

Outputs under ignored `artifacts/pacing/`: `source.json` records source/tool hashes and toolchain; `runs.json` contains active-time events and action walks; `summary.json` gives distributions and paired counterexamples. `--output` changes the output directory; seeds are bounded to 1–200 and duration to 10–600 seconds. Run twice into separate directories to compare reproducibility; UUID persistence suites are isolated and removed after each round.

Each seed runs A/B × largest-clear/height-safety/trace-oriented × full-motion/quiet presentation models. All policies observe only the committed visible board. They react after 450 ms, tap immediately after reaction, or visit/release trace steps at 120 ms each. These are explicit plausible assumptions, not measured human timings. Traces use deterministic full-component walks with retracing; the runner does not enumerate optimal subsets or simulate finger coordinates. Tie-breaking is deterministic. Height-safety weights removed cells by squared visible column height; it cannot see hidden incoming columns.

Store scheduling, input gates, deferrals and accepted commits execute real code. Presenter completion uses source-derived 0.15 s quiet, 0.56 s clear, and `0.48 + 0.09 × (arrivals−1) + 0.34` s arrival durations; these are **models**, not SpriteKit measurements. Terminal presentation is not validated. Rain wins exact-time ties. The synchronous observer only records immutable events; gesture cancellation is applied outside callbacks. Cancellation counts require an active real trace intent plus an automatic-shower invalidation, never ticket invalidation alone.

Active time includes presentation gates and stops at terminal/horizon. No-opportunity time counts only input-enabled intervals with no ordinary tap or legal traced component; deliberate policy waiting is separate. Rates divide accepted actions by active time. Early gaps end at 30 active seconds. Delivered intervals include opening; scheduled intervals retain their phase in the event log. Headroom is measured before arrivals and must be read alongside actual growth/overflow, since zero space can accept growth. Dark-blocked cells are a maximum simultaneous count; growth counts delivered growth events. Zero paused/manual/menu/background time in the persona comparison is intentional; those modes are not pooled with automatic play. Focused checks separately cover overdue deferral, suspension, stale trace tickets and fixed-action generation parity.

Every committed action is replayed under both cadence lookups and compared with the measured Store state. This verifies exact action-log generation parity; naturally differing actions can change relief and later incoming streams despite matching seeds. Source changes invalidate the recorded hashes. Native animation, touch, settings UI, VoiceOver, physical device and human preference remain separate acceptance. See [pacing evidence](../../docs/evidence/pacing-tools-2026-10-17/README.md).

## Parked: supernova candidates

These are isolated rule experiments, not app features or adopted power-ups. No new runtime flag, save field, asset or dependency is added. Run `python3 tools/mechanics/run-supernova.py` for matching-identity recoloring, or add `--rotate` for cyclic identity rotation. Both execute fixture regressions and 200-seed samples before emitting JSON. The candidate threshold is five starlight; ordinary clears retain the three-starlight threshold. Recolor only orthogonal surviving colored neighbors of the original group before settling. Preserve survivor IDs and growth, ordinary Dusk removal, score conservation, preview and RNG. Never cascade. Rotation follows the existing `Jewel.allCases` order and is a diagnostic rule, not an approved character transformation order.

Policies clear every available group, choosing the first canonical group. They compare ordinary tap, charge whenever eligible, and charge only when it creates more immediately available groups. This last policy examines committed hypothetical states; it does not simulate a person's comprehension, finger timing, preview or charge gesture. Counts across policies have different visited states and denominators. `differingFollowups` compares settled boards; better/worse counts compare legal-group counts, not eventual survival or enjoyment. Equal group counts can conceal strategically different boards. Each committed state is checked for valid-save invariants; every clear is round-tripped and its next shower compared after restore.

The depth metrics also compare total immediately clearable starlight and overflow through the already announced shower with no intervening clear. `tapExamples` retains up to three exact pre-input states from the ordinary-tap trajectory where tapping preserves more clearable value or avoids immediate overflow. These are replay fixtures, not normal app saves. Future unseen previews may differ between branches. Zero observed overflow differences is not a proof of equivalence or lack of strategic depth.

The October 17 sample shows stronger immediate follow-ups overwhelmingly favor charging; neither establishes a sufficient reason to prefer a tap on eligible groups. Resolve the tradeoff and preview/comprehension design before adoption. The interaction lab below is parked following the user's shower-variety choice.
