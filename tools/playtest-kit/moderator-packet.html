Optional R1 prompt, after replay questions

  1. Explain the five-kind legend and top-down coordinates. Provide a usable large-print or spoken form if needed; if access remains inadequate, mark unavailable.
  2. Show only the participant card. Ask its three questions without indicating a preferred group. Record the prediction before showing an outcome or answer.
  3. If needed, ask: “What could you guarantee from the pieces already here over two moves?” Mark this as an extra planning prompt, not spontaneous strategy.
  4. Offer the same optional explanation: “Survivors fall straight down in their own columns and can join matching neighbors. New refill pieces are unknown. A new group needs another tap and another move.” Record acceptance, prior exposure and any other help.

R1 is a supplementary reachable-board probe. It does not replace the protocol's A→B transfer pair or its scoring. Repeating R1 after explanation is not evidence of transfer. If used after A/B, record that prior teaching. Do not change the protocol's thresholds based on this card.
